# Brand notes — Valden

**Name:** Valden. Short, neutral, pronounceable in English, Hebrew and European languages; no
existing-brand claims were checked — run a trademark search before adopting it.

**Positioning:** a distribution desk for business buyers. Credible, precise, unhurried. Copy states
what the platform does and what it cannot promise (stock, delivery dates, PayPal limits).

**Visual direction:** freight and trade documents — manifests, crate markings, customs labels.

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep water | `#13335E` | Brand surface, primary text headings, primary buttons on light |
| Paper | `#F4F6F8` | Page background (cool, not cream) |
| Steel | `#25303B` | Body text |
| Line | `#D6DDE3` | Borders and dividers |
| Customs tag | `#F2C230` | Primary call to action only; never for decoration |

**Type:** Archivo variable (self-hosted). Expanded width (`font-stretch: 125%`, weight 800) for display
headings — the stencilled-crate signature; normal width for body; tabular figures for all money and
quantities.

**Mark:** three freight units on a pallet line; the upper unit in customs yellow. Favicon in
`public/favicon.svg`.

**Signature element:** the homepage "catalog manifest" — a live table of product lines, listing
counts and minimum order quantities drawn from the database. No invented statistics.
